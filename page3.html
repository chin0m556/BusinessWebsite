Our Origin Story

Founded inside a university robotics lab, three students got tired of traditional auto shops refusing to work on custom electric drivetrains. With a shared interest in technology and automotive innovation, AEK Electric Auto Repairs was created to provide service options for the next generation of electric vehicles.

Today, our fictional business represents a team focused on dependable service, modern technology, and helping EV owners feel confident about vehicle maintenance.
